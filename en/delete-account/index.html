Deleting your account

Radni Sati · Adnan Hodžić

You can delete your Radni Sati account yourself, in the app. If you cannot use the app, send us a request by email.

1. In the app (worker and foreman)

  1. Open Radni Sati and sign in.
  2. At the bottom of the screen (for workers: “Moji sati” / My hours) tap “Obriši nalog” (Delete account).
  3. Read what is deleted, type the word OBRIŠI and tap “Obriši moj nalog” (Delete my account).

If there are hours on the phone that have not been sent yet, the app first asks you to send them, so they are not lost.

2. Without the app

Send an email from the address you sign in with to trmadagaskar@gmail.com, with the subject “Delete account” and the company name. We cannot confirm a request from another address, so we will ask for additional verification.

Send request by email

We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and tell you when it is done.

3. Company owner

An owner cannot delete their account in the app while the company exists, because workers would lose the record of their hours and payments. Send a request by email from the owner account’s address and tell us:

  • whether we should delete only your account (for example when someone else takes over the company) or your account and all company data;
  • whether you want a copy of all company data before deletion.

Before company data is deleted, check how long the company is required by law to keep work and payment records. That is the company’s responsibility.

What is deleted and what stays

DataWhat happens
Email and passworddeleted immediately; signing in is no longer possible
Sign-ins on devicesall devices are signed out
Name and phone in the profiledeleted immediately
Company membershipdeleted immediately
Unused sign-in codesrevoked
Working hours, payments, bonuses and deductionsstay with the company, under the name the owner entered, because the company needs them for pay and its legal obligations. They are deleted when the owner asks for the company to be deleted.
Change logstays with the company, as part of the same record
Backupsdeleted data disappears from backups after 12 weeks at the latest

If you want only some data deleted (for example a wrongly entered phone number) and not the whole account, contact your company or us at the same address.
