Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

Radni Sati (“Work Hours”) is an app in which small companies and construction crews keep a record of working hours, sites and payments to workers. This page explains what data the app keeps, who can see it, how long we keep it and how it is deleted.

The app is developed and maintained by Adnan Hodžić, Doboj Istok, Bosnia and Herzegovina (“we”). Contact: trmadagaskar@gmail.com.

In short

  • We keep only what a company and its people enter in the app: accounts, names, hours, sites and payments.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we do not sell or share data for profit.
  • Hourly rates and payments are visible only to the company owner and to the worker they concern. Foremen cannot see them.
  • You can delete your account yourself in the app. Hours and payments stay with the company, which needs them for its pay records.

Who is responsible for the data

The company (employer) that uses Radni Sati decides whom it adds and what it records about work and payments. The company is responsible for that data. We store and process it only for that company and for no other purpose.

We are responsible for account data (email, password) and for running the service itself.

If you are a worker with a question about your hours or payments, contact your company first. We will help when the company or you ask us.

The app does not collect location, contacts, photos, health data or advertising IDs. It uses no analytics or tracking tools.

Data on the phone

So that it works without internet, the app keeps on the phone: the sign-in, the chosen language, the company data needed for entry (name, sites) and hours not yet sent. Unsent hours wait on the phone until they are sent, and only by the account that entered them. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Why we use it

  • To make the app work: sign-in, entering and approving hours, calculating earnings and balance (hours × rate + bonuses − deductions − payments).
  • So the company has an accurate, verifiable record: payments are never deleted but reversed with a reason, and changes are logged.
  • For security and fixing errors.

We do not use data for advertising, do not build profiles of people and do not sell it.

Who can see the data

  • A worker sees their own hours, earnings, payments and balance.
  • A foreman sees workers’ names and hours, to approve them. Not hourly rates or payments.
  • The owner sees all data of their company.
  • Nobody from another company can see your company’s data. These rules are enforced by the database itself, not only by the app.
  • We access data only when needed: for maintenance, backups, fixing an error, or at the request of a company or user.

Service providers

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.): database and sign-in. Data is stored in a data centre in Frankfurt (Germany, European Union).
  • Google Play: app download, covered by Google’s privacy policy.

When an owner shares a sign-in code (for example via Viber or WhatsApp), they choose the app used to send it, and that message is covered by that app’s rules.

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires us to.

How long we keep data

  • Account: until you delete it. Deletion immediately removes the email, password, sign-ins on all devices, the name and phone in the profile, and the company membership.
  • Company records (hours, payments, bonuses, deductions, sites, the worker list with the name and phone the owner entered, the change log): while the company uses Radni Sati. They stay when a worker deletes their account, because the company needs them for pay and for its legal obligations. The owner can ask for the company to be deleted in the app (or by email): its data is deleted 7 days after the request, and within 30 days at the latest. Until then the owner can take the request back and save a copy of all data in the app.
  • Sign-in codes are valid for 7 days and can be used only once.
  • Backups are made once a week and the last 12 are kept (about 3 months). Deleted data disappears from backups after 12 weeks at the latest.
  • Technical logs at the database provider are kept briefly, under its rules.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords and codes are not stored in readable form.
  • Role-based access (owner, foreman, worker) is enforced by the database for every row.
  • Accounts cannot be created without a code issued by the company.

No system is completely secure. If we learn of a data breach, we will inform the companies concerned and act as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data. You see your hours, earnings and payments in the app. To correct hours or payments, contact your company, which keeps them. For anything else write to trmadagaskar@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

If you believe your data is not handled properly, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Agency of Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Deleting your account

You delete your account in the app: “Obriši nalog” (Delete account) at the bottom of the main screen. If you cannot use the app, or you are a company owner, see the account deletion page.

Children

Radni Sati is meant for companies and their workers. It is not meant for children.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, the new version is published on this page with a new date. We also announce important changes in the app.
